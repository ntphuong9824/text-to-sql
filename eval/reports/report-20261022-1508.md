### h05 (single, hard, en)

- Question: For each year from 2022 to 2025, show revenue and the difference from the previous year's revenue
- Status: OK
- Note: result differs from gold: gold 4 rows x 3 cols, generated 4 rows x 3 cols

Gold SQL:

```sql
WITH yearly AS (
  SELECT d.year AS year, SUM(f.revenue) AS revenue
  FROM dw.fact_sales f
  JOIN dw.dim_date d ON d.date_key = f.date_key
  GROUP BY d.year
), diffs AS (
  SELECT y.year, y.revenue, y.revenue - LAG(y.revenue) OVER (ORDER BY y.year) AS revenue_change
  FROM yearly y
)
SELECT x.year, x.revenue, x.revenue_change
FROM diffs x
WHERE x.year BETWEEN 2022 AND 2025
```
